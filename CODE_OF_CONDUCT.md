# Code of Conduct

## Our Goal
We want to keep this project a friendly and welcoming place for everyone. Whether you're a seasoned developer or just starting out, your presence and contributions are valued.

## Our Standards
To ensure a positive experience for everyone, we ask that you follow these simple principles:

*   **Be Kind and Respectful**: Treat everyone with dignity. We're all here to learn and build something useful.
*   **Be Constructive**: When providing feedback or reviewing code, focus on the work and the solution, not the person.
*   **Be Helpful**: If you see someone struggling with a setup or a bug, offer a hand if you can.
*   **Maintain Professionalism**: Avoid language or behavior that could be seen as harassing, exclusionary, or inflammatory.

## Reporting Issues
If you encounter behavior that doesn't align with these values, please reach out to the maintainer(s). We want to keep this a safe and productive space for everyone.

Thank you for being part of the community!
